About

About Francesco.

I'm Francesco Italiano, an Italian tattoo artist based in Coleford. I started tattooing in 1999, and over more than two decades I've worked across a wide range of styles. Realism is where I feel most at home, whether I'm building depth in black and grey or working with strong colour.

I previously taught tattooing and hygiene at the International Academy in Milan. My work also includes cover-ups and tattooing over scars. Each piece starts with a conversation about your idea and how it will sit on your body.

Studio

The studio.

I'm Francesco Italiano. I design custom tattoos around the shape of the body and the way the piece will sit on the skin. Tell me what you have in mind, where you want it and roughly how big it should be.
